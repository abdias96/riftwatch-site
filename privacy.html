Riftwatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Riftwatch is an event-timer app. It is built so that it does not need to know who you are.

What we collect

Nothing. Riftwatch has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your region, reminder settings, energy value, boss timers and checklist are stored only on your device (and in your iCloud/device backups if you use them). Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network requests

The app downloads a public event-schedule file from abdias96.github.io so that event times can be corrected without an app update. That request contains no personal information. Like any web server, it may briefly record the IP address in standard access logs, which are not used to identify you.

Children

Riftwatch is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: riftwatch@usacentavo.com.

Riftwatch is an unofficial fan tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCSOFT.

Riftwatch — Política de privacidad

No recopilamos nada. Riftwatch no tiene cuentas, analítica, publicidad ni rastreo.
