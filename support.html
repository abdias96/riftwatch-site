Riftwatch support

Need help, or are event times off for your region? Email riftwatch@usacentavo.com with your region and what you see in game. Schedule corrections are published without an app update.

Frequently asked

Reminders don't arrive. Open Settings → Notifications → Riftwatch and allow notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work without internet; open the app at least once every couple of days so it can schedule the next ones.

Times are wrong. Check the region in the top-right corner. Regions marked “unconfirmed” are still being verified.

Lock Screen countdown. Swipe left on the Rift row (or long-press it) and choose “Show countdown on Lock Screen”. It also starts by itself when you open the app within 20 minutes of a rift.
